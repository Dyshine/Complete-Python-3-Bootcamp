      About this library

      Stillroom is a private, local film-still library. Everything here lives in one folder on your computer; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

      Where the stills come from

      • Film frames: one frame per shot, cut from video files you added (films you own, or public-domain and Creative Commons films).
      • TMDB stills: official stills downloaded from The Movie Database for personal, non-commercial use. They are kept for at most 6 months.
