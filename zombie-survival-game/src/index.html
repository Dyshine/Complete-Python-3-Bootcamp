    DEAD ACRE

    You are on death row, and you just volunteered for television. The network drops condemned inmates into infected quarantine zones and broadcasts every minute. There is no finish line: the show runs until you die, and every night you last is another episode. Scavenge the ghost towns, hunt, take jobs, board up your cabin and play to the cameras: an excited audience brings sponsor drops, and a bored one brings trouble.

    Brute (night 5). Charges and knocks you flying.
