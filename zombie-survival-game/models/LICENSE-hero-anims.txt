hero_anims.json: animation clips from "Universal Animation Library" by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), CC0 1.0
(public domain). Source: https://opengameart.org/content/universal-animation-library
Retargeted onto the hero's skeleton and baked to per-bone quaternions at 30 fps (idle, walk, jog, sprint, crouch,
hit, death, sword swing, punches, kneeling repair, pick-up, interact, pistol shoot and reload, swim and tread water).
