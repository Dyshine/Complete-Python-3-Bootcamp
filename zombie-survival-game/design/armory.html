Dead Acre Armory

Nine weapon classes, each in four quality tiers, plus nine special weapons. Every gun can be broken down into parts that feed mods and tier upgrades at the base workshop. Tier 2 is today's in-game gun, so the numbers slot straight into the current balance.

Quality tiers

Colour-coded like the loot crates. A tier changes damage, handling, mod slots and how the gun looks, all at once.

Firepower at a glance

Sustained damage per second while firing (not counting reloads). Shotgun assumes every pellet hits; launcher is per target in the blast.

Weapon classes

Pick a class to see all four tiers, what the gun breaks down into, and which mods it takes. Noise is how far away a horde hears the shot.

Breaking weapons down

Salvage any gun at the workshop. You get scrap and gun parts (the two currencies the game already has) plus six components. Better tiers give more back.

Mods

Crafted from components and fitted into a gun's mod slots. Tier 1 has no slots, Tier 2 has one, Tier 3 two, Tier 4 three.

Upgrading a tier

Done at the workshop. The hunting rifle and grenade launcher cost 30% more.

Special weapons

One of a kind, orange-tier. Each has a mechanic no other weapon has, and most live in a particular kind of building. Salvaging one gives a Relic Core, which upgrades another special from Mk I to Mk III.

Shots to kill

Body shots, no mods. Horde members get 4 HP tougher each night, so a Field gun that two-shots them early needs three by night 10.
