    Known infected

    Walker. Slow, relentless, chews through wooden walls.

    Runner (night 2). Fast and fragile. Don't let them flank you.

    Spitter (night 3). Lobs acid over your walls from range.

    Boomer (night 4). Explodes on contact. Shreds wall sections.

    Brute (night 5). Charges and knocks you flying.

    The Abomination (every 5th night). Ground slams and calls runners.
